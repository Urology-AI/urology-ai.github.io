# ePSA — Electronic PSA Tool

ePSA is a guided prostate cancer screening assessment for men and their clinicians. It walks a man through his personal risk profile and, if he has them, his PSA and MRI results, and turns them into a plain-language result he can bring to a urology visit. It is a starting point for a conversation, not a diagnosis.

ePSA meets men wherever they are in their prostate health journey:

1. **Should I get a PSA test?** For men who have never had a PSA test. A short questionnaire on guideline risk factors, symptoms, and lifestyle.
2. **I have a PSA result.** Adds the PSA value and interprets it against age-adjusted AUA/SUO 2026 guidance.
3. **I had a PSA and an MRI.** Adds the MRI PI-RADS score and guides the biopsy conversation.

Every question is labelled by where it comes from. **Guideline-based** factors (AUA/SUO and NCCN), such as age, ancestry, family history, and inherited gene variants, are kept separate from **research-based** factors, such as urinary symptoms, BMI, exercise, smoking, diet, and sexual health.

ePSA is part of the **Million Strong Men** initiative to make prostate cancer screening accessible to every man.
